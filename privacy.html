MagicClips Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

MagicClips is a personal desktop app that runs on the owner's own computer. It turns long videos into short vertical clips and, when the owner chooses, posts those clips to their own social media accounts. This policy explains what information the app uses and where it goes.

1. Who operates MagicClips

MagicClips is operated by JJ Valle for personal use. It is not a public service, and it has no user accounts, website or servers of its own.

2. Information the app stores

Everything is stored only on the computer where MagicClips is installed:

  • Videos you upload or link to, and the clips made from them.
  • Transcripts of those videos.
  • Titles, captions and posting schedules you create.
  • Login tokens for the social accounts you connect (TikTok, YouTube, Instagram), and an optional browser cookies file used only to download videos you link to.

The app does not run analytics, tracking or advertising, and it does not collect information about anyone other than its owner.

3. Information received from TikTok

When you connect a TikTok account, MagicClips receives:

  • Your basic profile information (display name), used only to show which account is connected.
  • An access token and a refresh token, used only to upload videos you have chosen to post.

MagicClips does not read your followers, messages, likes, viewing history or any other TikTok data.

4. Where information is sent

Information leaves your computer only in these cases:

  • TikTok, YouTube (Google) and Instagram (Meta): the video, title and caption you schedule are uploaded to your own account through each platform's official API when you post. Each platform's own privacy policy applies to content once it is uploaded.
  • Anthropic (Claude API): video transcripts are sent to Anthropic's API to choose clip moments and draft titles and captions. Anthropic's privacy policy applies to that processing.
  • Video websites: when you paste a link, the app downloads that video from the site it is hosted on.

MagicClips never sells, rents or shares your information with anyone else.

5. Keeping or deleting your information

  • Disconnecting an account in the app's Accounts tab deletes that account's stored tokens.
  • You can also revoke MagicClips' access at any time in your TikTok settings under Security → Manage app permissions, or the equivalent page on Google or Meta.
  • Deleting the app's data folder permanently removes all stored videos, clips, transcripts, schedules and tokens.

6. Security

Tokens and files are kept on your local computer and protected by your computer's own security. They are not uploaded to any MagicClips server, because there is none.

7. Children

MagicClips is not intended for anyone under 18 and does not knowingly process information about children.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change too.
